A route that wins on $50 can lose on $5,000 — the winning pool fills up and price impact eats the edge. This quotes the same trade at eight sizes against every route and finds the point where the leader changes. Live mainnet quotes, read-only.

Why this exists: routecheck showed that a direct SHIB/USDC pool quoted 0.55 USDC where SHIB→WETH→USDC paid 5.54 USDC. That told me which route was wrong — not how much I could push through the right one. Size and route are the same question asked twice. Part of Tallow.
